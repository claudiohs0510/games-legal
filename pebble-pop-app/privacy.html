Pebble Pop: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026.

What the app collects

Pebble Pop does not ask for an account and does not read your contacts, location, photos or microphone. The only data that leaves your phone is what the ad service below collects, and only when an ad is requested.

What stays on your phone

Your board progress, best score and sound settings are saved on your phone only. Deleting the app removes them; there is no cloud save.

Ads

Pebble Pop shows full-screen ads between games: after every lost game, once the first few games and the first few minutes of play have passed. After a game ends you can also choose to watch a short video ad for a "second chance" on the board; that is optional and never required. The ads come from Google AdMob. Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location, and how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads. On iPhone the app first asks your permission to track you across other apps and websites; if you say no, ads still work (the second chance works the same) but are not personalised. In some regions Google also shows a consent form. See Google's privacy policy for what Google does with this data. You can reset or limit tracking in iPhone Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking. There is no other analytics.

Children

Pebble Pop is not made for children under 13 and is not in the Kids category.

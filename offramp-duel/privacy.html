Offramp: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026.

Offramp is a puzzle game. We do not run any server for it and we do not ask you for an account, a name or an email address.

What stays on your phone

Your level progress, your stars, the maze you are drawing and your sound setting are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Mazes you share

When you send a maze, the game turns it into a short text code (and, after a duel, adds your time and number of taps). You choose where to paste or send it. That code contains only the maze and those two numbers, and it never passes through our servers.

Advertising

If you ask for an extra hint, the game can show an optional video ad from Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect device identifiers (including the advertising identifier if you allow tracking), coarse location derived from your IP address, ad interaction data and diagnostics. iOS asks you whether to allow tracking, and the game works the same if you say no. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Children

Offramp is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

One Line: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026.

What the app collects

One Line does not ask for an account and does not read your contacts, photos or microphone. The game itself does not collect your name, email or location. The only data that leaves your phone is what the ad service below collects.

What stays on your phone

Your level stars, the level you are on and your sound setting are saved on your phone only. Deleting the app removes them; there is no cloud save.

Ads

One Line is free and shows a full-screen ad after every third level you solve. The ads come from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, your IP address and the approximate location it implies, how you interact with ads, and basic diagnostics from the ad software.

On iPhone the app first asks whether it may track you across other apps and websites. If you say no, ads still appear but are not personalised, and the game works exactly the same. In some regions Google also shows a consent form before ads. You can change your choice any time in iPhone Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking.

We do not sell your data and we do not receive the data Google collects. What Google does with it is described in Google's privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy) and on its page How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Analytics

There is no analytics in the game itself.

Children

One Line is not made for children under 13 and is not in the Kids category.

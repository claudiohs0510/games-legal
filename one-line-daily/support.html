FAQ

  • My stars disappeared. Stars are stored on the phone; deleting the app or clearing its data removes them. There is no account and no cloud save.
  • How the lines work. Touch a ring and drag through the cells. From level 65 there are two or three lines: each goes from its ring to the square of its colour, lines may not touch each other, and together they must fill every cell. Lift to pause, touch a line's glowing end (or its ring) to pick it again, drag back to step back.
  • The hint. It shows the next four cells of the line you picked and counts against the third star; it costs nothing otherwise.
  • Ads. One Line is free. A short full-screen ad appears after every third level you solve, never while you are drawing. If an ad cannot load, the game just continues.
  • The tracking question. On first launch iPhone asks if the app may track you. Either answer is fine: if you say no, ads are not personalised and nothing else changes. You can change it in Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking. See the privacy policy for details.
  • A bug. Say what happened, the level number, your phone model and system version.

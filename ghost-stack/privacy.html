Ghost Stack: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026.

Ghost Stack is a stacking game. We do not run any server for it and we do not ask you for an account or an email address.

What stays on your phone

Your level progress, stars, best runs (the tap timings used to show "your best" ghost), daily score, the name you type for your ghost and your sound and haptics settings are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Duel codes you share

When you finish a duel, the game makes a text code holding a random seed, the name you typed (optional, up to 12 characters) and the timing of each tap in your three towers. You choose where to paste or send it. It never passes through our servers.

Advertising

If you choose "Second chance" after a fall, the game shows an optional video ad from Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect device identifiers (including the advertising identifier if you allow tracking), coarse location derived from your IP address, ad interaction data and diagnostics. iOS asks whether to allow tracking; the game works the same if you say no. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Your choices about ads and tracking

You can change the tracking choice at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. If you are in a region where the game asks for consent to personalised ads (for example the EEA, the UK or Switzerland), you can review or withdraw that consent at any time with the "Ad privacy choices" button on Settings of Ghost Stack. If you allow no tracking, the game works the same and any ad you choose to watch is less personalised. If you decline, the game may show no ad at all; only the optional second chance is affected. The game uses Google's consent tool for this choice.

How long data is kept, and deleting it

Data saved on your phone stays until you delete the app, which removes it completely. We hold no copy and have no server, so there is nothing else for us to delete. Data collected by Google for advertising is kept and deleted under Google's own policy linked above; Google handles that data under its own privacy policy and its advertising terms. To ask Google about ad data, or to reset your advertising identifier, use iOS Settings or Google's controls at https://myadcenter.google.com. You can also write to us at the contact address below and we will help.

Who we share data with

We do not sell data and we do not share anything with anyone except as described under Advertising: the Google Mobile Ads SDK inside the app sends the data listed there to Google to show and measure the optional video ad.

Children

Ghost Stack is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Ghost Stack: Support

How do I play?

A block slides above your tower. Tap anywhere to drop it. The part that hangs over the block below falls off. Drop it right on top for a perfect; three perfects in a row grow the block back a little.

What is the ghost?

A replay of a real run on the same tower: your best run in Worlds and Daily, or your friend's run in a duel. It drops exactly when they did.

How do duels work?

Tap Duel a friend, play three short towers, then Send to a friend. They open Ghost Stack, tap Open a duel code and paste the whole message (or tap the ghoststack:// link). They race your ghost, best of three, and can send their code back so you see the result.

The code won't open.

Paste the whole message; codes start with GS-. Case, spaces and dashes don't matter, and O/0 or I/L/1 mix-ups are fixed for you. A checksum catches a mistyped or cut-off code instead of loading the wrong duel.

What do the worlds change?

Ember Wind: the block is fast going right and slow coming back. Drift Deck: the base sways. Last Light: a narrow base with wind and sway.

Ads.

Only an optional video when you choose Second chance after a fall in Worlds or Daily, once per tower. Never in duels.

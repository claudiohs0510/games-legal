FAQ

  • My coins or shapes disappeared. Everything is stored on the phone; deleting the app or clearing its data removes it. There is no cloud save yet.
  • No sound. Check the silent switch and the Sound button in the header. Haptics still work with the phone on silent.
  • The free grant says 24h. The grant refills once per day; the button shows the hours left.
  • A bug. Say what happened, your iPhone model and iOS version, and whether the app was in the background.

Splashy Squeeze: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026.

What the app collects

Splashy Squeeze does not ask for an account and does not read your contacts, location, photos or microphone. Nothing leaves your phone.

What stays on your phone

Your coins, unlocked shapes, sound setting and squeeze count are saved on your phone only. Deleting the app removes them; there is no cloud save.

Ads and analytics

This version has no ads and no analytics. If rewarded video is added later, this policy will be updated before that build ships.

Children

Splashy Squeeze is not made for children under 13 and is not in the Kids category.

FAQ

  • My progress disappeared. Progress is stored on the phone; deleting the app or clearing its data removes it. There is no cloud save yet.
  • Offline earnings. The garden keeps growing while you are away, capped at 8 hours. The welcome banner shows what you earned.
  • Sound. Toggle it in the header; the setting is saved.
  • A bug. Say what happened, your iPhone model and iOS version, and whether the app was in the background.
